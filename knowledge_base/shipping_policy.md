# KovaiCart – Shipping and Delivery Policy

**Document:** shipping_policy.md  
**Company:** KovaiCart  
**Business:** Fictional Indian e-commerce retailer  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Currency:** Indian Rupees (INR / ₹)

## 1. General Shipping Information

KovaiCart delivers to serviceable pin codes across India.

- Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days after successful payment.
- Standard delivery normally takes 3–7 business days after dispatch.
- Express delivery, where available, normally takes 1–3 business days after dispatch.
- Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays observed by the relevant delivery location.
- Delivery estimates may vary by destination, courier availability, weather and other operational disruptions.
- Customers receive an order confirmation after successful order placement.
- A tracking link is sent by SMS or email after the order is dispatched.

## 2. Shipping Charges

- Standard shipping: ₹49 per order.
- Express shipping: ₹99 per order, where available.
- Free standard shipping on orders with a merchandise value of ₹999 or more.
- Express shipping charges are not waived by the free-shipping threshold.
- Any applicable shipping charge is displayed at checkout before payment.

## 3. Order Tracking

**Q: How can I track my order?**

1. Sign in to the KovaiCart website or app.
2. Open **My Orders**.
3. Select the relevant order.
4. Select **Track Order** to view the latest available courier status.

Customers can also use the tracking link sent by SMS or email after dispatch.

**Q: I have not received a tracking link. What should I do?**

- Tracking information is generated after dispatch, not immediately after order placement.
- Allow up to 2 business days for order processing.
- If the order has not been dispatched after 2 business days, contact customer support with the order ID.

## 4. Delayed Deliveries

**Q: My order has not arrived within the estimated delivery period.**

- Check the latest status using the tracking link.
- If the tracking status has not changed for more than 3 business days, contact customer support.
- Support will review the shipment status and, where necessary, raise an enquiry with the courier.
- A courier enquiry normally takes up to 3 business days for an initial update.

A delay does not automatically qualify an order for a refund. The shipment must be reviewed under the applicable order and refund process.

**Q: The tracking page says delivered, but I have not received the parcel.**

1. Check the delivery location and any delivery instructions.
2. Check with household members, reception/security staff or neighbours, where applicable.
3. Review any delivery confirmation or proof provided by the courier.
4. If the parcel is still missing, contact KovaiCart support with the order ID.

Support will investigate the delivery with the courier. Do not promise a refund or replacement before the investigation is completed.

## 5. Incorrect Delivery Address

- Customers should review their address carefully before placing an order.
- An address correction may be requested before dispatch by contacting support.
- Once an order has been dispatched, an address change cannot be guaranteed.
- If the courier offers a supported redirection option, support will explain the available next steps.

## 6. Potentially Lost Shipments

A shipment may be treated as potentially lost when:
- Tracking has not updated for more than 5 business days; or
- The courier confirms that the parcel cannot be located.

KovaiCart support will raise or review a courier investigation. A refund or replacement will be considered after the investigation and order verification.

## 7. Partial Deliveries

If an order contains multiple items, items may sometimes be dispatched separately.

- Check the order page for item-level shipment information.
- If an item is marked delivered but is missing, contact support with the order ID and missing item details.
- Support will verify the shipment before confirming a resolution.

## 8. Contact Information

Email: support@kovaicart.example  
Support hours: Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00 IST.

Customers should include their order ID when contacting support.

## 9. Important Restrictions

- Delivery timelines are estimates, not guaranteed delivery appointments.
- Delivery may be affected by courier operations, weather, public holidays or serviceability.
- The AI support system must not invent live tracking updates or claim to have contacted a courier.
- The AI support system must not promise a refund or replacement solely because an order is late.
- Legal threats, suspected fraud and payment disputes must be escalated to human support.
