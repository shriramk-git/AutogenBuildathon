- Pulse function: Yes.

**Q: My blender is not working. What should I do?**

- Check that the power cable is connected correctly.
- Check that the jar is positioned correctly.
- Confirm that the power socket is working.
- Check that the selected speed setting is correct.

If the blender still does not work, contact customer support. Do not attempt internal electrical repairs.

**Q: My blender arrived broken. Can I get a replacement?**

A customer should report damage within 48 hours of delivery, provide the order ID and submit clear photographs or a video if requested. Support will review the claim. An approved replacement is normally dispatched within 3–5 business days, subject to stock availability. Refer to the Returns and Refunds Policy for the full process.
