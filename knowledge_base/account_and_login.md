# KovaiCart – Customer Account and Login Support Guide

**Document:** account_and_login.md  
**Company:** KovaiCart  
**Business:** Fictional Indian e-commerce retailer  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026

## 1. Creating a Customer Account

**Q: How do I create a KovaiCart account?**

1. Open the KovaiCart website or app.
2. Select **Register**.
3. Enter your name and mobile number or email address.
4. Create a password.
5. Accept the terms and conditions.
6. Submit the registration form.
7. Complete the verification step using the OTP or email link provided.

An account becomes active after the required verification is completed.

## 2. Forgotten Password

**Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?**

1. Open the KovaiCart login page.
2. Select **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter the registered mobile number or email address.
4. Request the verification OTP or reset link.
5. Enter the OTP or open the reset link.
6. Create and confirm a new password.
7. Return to the login page and sign in.

A password reset link, if issued, expires after 30 minutes. An OTP expires after 5 minutes.

**Q: I did not receive the OTP or reset email.**

- Confirm that the registered mobile number or email address was entered correctly.
- Check the SMS inbox and, for email, the spam or junk folder.
- Wait up to 5 minutes before requesting another OTP.
- Check that the mobile device has network coverage and can receive SMS.
- If the problem continues, contact customer support.

Never share an OTP or password with another person, including someone claiming to be a support representative.

## 3. Password Requirements

A KovaiCart password must:
- Contain at least 8 characters.
- Include at least one uppercase letter.
- Include at least one lowercase letter.
- Include at least one number.
- Include at least one special character.

Customers should use a unique password and must not share it.

## 4. Account Temporarily Locked

**Q: My account is locked. What should I do?**

- An account is temporarily locked after 5 consecutive unsuccessful login attempts.
- The automatic lock period is 30 minutes.
- Wait 30 minutes before trying again.
- If the account remains locked after that period, contact customer support.

Support must never ask the customer to disclose their password or OTP.

## 5. Changing a Registered Mobile Number or Email

**Q: How can I change my registered mobile number or email address?**

1. Sign in to the KovaiCart account.
2. Open **My Account**.
3. Select **Profile Settings**.
4. Choose the mobile number or email field to update.
5. Enter the new contact detail.
6. Complete the verification step sent to the new contact detail.

The change is completed only after successful verification.

If the customer cannot access the account or the old contact detail, human support must review the identity-verification process. The AI must not bypass verification.

## 6. Updating Personal Information

Customers can review or update available profile information from **My Account**, including:
- Name.
- Mobile number.
- Email address.
- Saved delivery addresses.

An address change in the account does not automatically change the delivery address of an order that has already been placed. Refer to the Shipping Policy for address-change requests relating to an existing order.

## 7. Suspicious Account Activity

**Q: I see an order that I did not place.**

This may indicate unauthorised account access.

Customers should:
1. Change their password immediately if they can safely access the account.
2. Contact KovaiCart support and report the suspicious activity.
3. Provide the relevant order ID or transaction reference, if available.
4. Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, PINs or full payment-card details.

Suspected account compromise, identity misuse, unauthorised purchases or fraud must be escalated to human support.

## 8. OTP and Account Security

- KovaiCart uses OTP or email verification for supported account-registration and account-change flows.
- OTPs are intended only for the customer who requested them.
- Support representatives will never ask customers to disclose their OTP or password.
- Customers should not click suspicious links claiming to be KovaiCart login or payment pages.

## 9. Account Deactivation

Customers who wish to deactivate their account must contact customer support.

The support team will review the request and explain the next steps. The AI support system must not claim that an account has been deactivated or that personal data has been deleted.

## 10. Contact Information

Email: support@kovaicart.example  
Support hours: Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00 IST.

For a security incident, include **Account Security** in the support request subject.

## 11. Security Restrictions

- Never ask customers for passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, CVVs or banking passwords.
- Never ask customers to share a password-reset link.
- Escalate suspected fraud, identity theft and unauthorised account access.
- Do not claim to have changed account details or unlocked an account unless an authorised system has confirmed the action.
