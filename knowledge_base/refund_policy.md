# KovaiCart – Returns, Refunds and Cancellation Policy

**Document:** refund_policy.md  
**Company:** KovaiCart  
**Business:** Fictional Indian e-commerce retailer  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Currency:** Indian Rupees (INR / ₹)

## 1. General Return Policy

KovaiCart accepts eligible return requests within 7 calendar days of delivery.

To qualify for a standard return:
- The product must be unused and in resalable condition.
- Original packaging, tags, manuals and included accessories must be returned.
- The customer must provide the order ID or proof of purchase.
- The product must be eligible for return under its product-page conditions.

Some product categories may have additional restrictions. If the product page states a category-specific restriction, support must review that condition before confirming eligibility.

## 2. How to Request a Return

**Q: How do I return a product?**

1. Sign in to the KovaiCart website or app.
2. Open **My Orders**.
3. Select the relevant order and item.
4. Select **Request Return**, if available.
5. Choose the reason for return and provide the requested details.
6. Submit the request and wait for approval and return instructions.
7. Pack the product securely and follow the return instructions provided by KovaiCart.

Customers should not send a product to an address found online or dispatch a return before receiving the return instructions.

## 3. Refund Processing

**Q: How long does a refund take?**

- After the returned product is received, KovaiCart performs an inspection.
- Inspection and refund approval normally take 2–3 business days after receipt.
- Once approved, the refund is initiated to the original payment method.
- After initiation, banks and payment providers may take 5–10 business days to reflect the amount.
- The time taken to display the refund depends on the bank or payment provider.

**Q: Can I receive the refund in cash or to a different bank account?**

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method. Any exception must be reviewed and approved by the support team. The AI support system must not promise an alternative payment destination.

## 4. Return Shipping Charges

- For an approved change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for the return shipping cost unless the return instructions state otherwise.
- If the product is confirmed to be damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied, KovaiCart will arrange or reimburse the return shipping as instructed by support.
- Customers should contact support before returning a damaged or incorrect product.

## 5. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

**Q: I received a damaged or defective product. What should I do?**

1. Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery.
2. Provide the order ID and identify the affected item.
3. Describe the damage or defect.
4. Attach clear photographs or a short video showing the issue, if requested.
5. Wait for the support team to review the claim and provide instructions.

After verification, KovaiCart may offer:
- A replacement, subject to stock availability; or
- A refund if a replacement is unavailable or the support team approves a refund.

For an approved replacement, dispatch normally takes 3–5 business days after approval, subject to stock availability.

Customers should keep the product and packaging until support provides instructions. Do not promise a replacement or refund before verification.

## 6. Order Cancellation

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**

- An order may be cancelled before it is dispatched.
- Sign in, open **My Orders**, select the order and use **Cancel Order** if the option is available.
- If the cancellation option is unavailable, contact support immediately.
- A dispatched order cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.
- After delivery, the customer may submit a return request if the item is eligible under this policy.

If a cancellation is accepted before dispatch and payment has already been collected, the refund is initiated to the original payment method. The normal bank/payment-provider processing time applies.

## 7. Non-Returnable or Restricted Situations

A standard change-of-mind return may be declined when:
- The request is made after the 7-calendar-day return window.
- The product has been used, damaged by the customer or is not in resalable condition.
- Essential accessories, tags or components are missing.
- The product page identifies the item as non-returnable.

A restriction on change-of-mind returns does not automatically prevent a customer from reporting a genuine damage, defect or incorrect-item issue. Such cases must be reviewed by support.

## 8. Duplicate, Failed or Incorrect Payments

**Q: I was charged more than once for one order. What should I do?**

- Do not attempt to make another payment until the transaction status is checked.
- Contact support with the order ID, transaction reference and amount(s).
- Do not send a full card number, CVV, PIN or banking password.
- Support must verify the payment records and payment-provider status before confirming whether a refund is due.

A duplicate charge, unauthorised transaction, suspected fraud or payment dispute must be escalated to human support. The AI support system must not guarantee a refund before verification.

## 10. Important Restrictions

- The AI support system must not guarantee a refund before the required checks are complete.
- The AI support system must not invent return windows, refund timelines or exceptions.
- Payment disputes, suspected fraud and legal threats must be escalated to human support.
- If a situation is not covered by this document, tell the customer that the policy does not provide enough information and refer the case to human support.
